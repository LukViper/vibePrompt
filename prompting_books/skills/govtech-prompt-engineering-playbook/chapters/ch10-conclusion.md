# Chapter 10: Conclusion

## Core Idea
This Beta playbook ends as a call to practice and share: prompt engineering compounds when taught to colleagues, not when read once.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Share-the-playbook loop**: If useful, spread it to others who would benefit.
  - When to use: After finishing tutorials or applying CO-STAR at work.
  - How: Point peers to the public playbook; officers use the LaunchPad contextualised edition.

## Key Concepts
- **Beta v3**: Living document (public edition altered for external consumption).
- **Practice over theory**: Tutorials and iteration beat passive reading.
- **Community amplification**: Skill spreads socially inside and outside agencies.

## Mental Models
- Use **teach-back** when Y = locking in CO-STAR and task prompts.
- Prefer **ongoing iteration** when Y = model behaviour drifts over time (as tutorials warn).

## Anti-patterns
- **One-and-done reading**: Without tutorials, frameworks stay abstract.
- **Hoarding prompts**: Teams move faster when patterns are shared and reviewed.

## Worked Example
After Tutorial 6, pick one real work artifact (citizen email, meeting notes, campaign blurb). Run it through CO-STAR + the matching task family, document Creativity used, and share the before/after with a teammate as a 10-minute brown-bag.

## Key Takeaways
1. You’ve covered concepts → CO-STAR → six tasks → advanced tips → drills.
2. Keep verifying outputs; models change.
3. Share what works.
4. You remain the star; the AI is the co-star.
