# Chapter 10: What to do? Next steps

## Core Idea
Specific tips will age out; **writing better prompts and constraints** remains durable—practice ChatGPT/Midjourney, adapt or be replaced by people who can, and protect mind/body while you upskill.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Durable skills vs perishable tips**: Prioritize prompt + constraint craft over memorizing UI-specific tricks.
  - When to use: Feeling anxious that yesterday’s tutorial is obsolete.
  - How: Keep practicing the anatomy (role, task, goal, constraints, expectations); re-learn product chrome as needed.
- **AI won’t replace you—AI-fluent humans might**: Competitive frame for career urgency without fatalism.
  - When to use: Motivating consistent practice.
  - How: Schedule hands-on time with ChatGPT and image tools (book cites Midjourney); evolve workflows deliberately.
- **Whole-person operating system**: Exercise, nutrition, sleep as non-optional alongside AI skill.
  - When to use: Screen time expands with more tools.
  - How: Treat wellbeing as equal priority; none of the AI skill matters if mind/body fail.
- **Structured learning ladder**: Free YouTube → live masterclass for depth.
  - When to use: Ebook finished but you want guided ChatGPT + Midjourney practice.
  - How: Channel `@AnshMehraAI`; Think School live masterclasses; contact `admin@anshmehra.com` for doubts.

## Key Concepts
- **Constraint-writing skill**: Ability to bound and sequence asks—called out as enduringly valuable.
- **Adaptation**: Career survival skill in a fast AI market.
- **Midjourney**: Paired with ChatGPT as a practice surface for multimodal AI fluency.
- **Live masterclass**: Think School offering for structured ChatGPT & Prompt Engineering learning.
- **Wellbeing triad**: Exercise, nutrition, sleep.

## Mental Models
- Prefer **practicing constraints** when tool tips feel outdated.
- Use the **human-with-AI vs human-without-AI** comparison to prioritize learning.
- Protect **sleep/exercise/nutrition** when tool FOMO pushes endless screen hours.
- Escalate from **ebook → free video → live cohort** as depth needs grow.
- Think of 2023+ as a **practice decade**, not a one-book finish line.

## Anti-patterns
- **Clinging to obsolete step lists**: Ignores the durable-skill message.
- **Ignoring AI practice because “my job is safe”**: Underestimates AI-fluent peers.
- **All screens, no recovery**: Undermines the cognition prompting requires.
- **Stopping at bookmarking courses**: Consumption without application.

## Worked Example
**Personal “next 30 days” plan after the ebook** (faithful to chapter intent):
1. **Daily (15–20 min)**: One framed prompt with explicit constraints in your real job domain; save before/after notes.
2. **3× week**: Midjourney (or current image tool) + ChatGPT combo for a small creative brief.
3. **Weekly**: Ch 8 resource practice + Ch 9 tool radar (30–40 min total).
4. **Non-negotiables**: Sleep target, 2–3 exercise sessions, basic nutrition—calendar them like prompts practice.
5. **Depth option**: Join a Think School-style live masterclass if self-study stalls; use free YouTube meanwhile.

**Failure mode**: Finishing the ebook, saving 40 tool links, never writing constrained prompts again.

## Key Takeaways
1. Tips expire; prompt and constraint skill compounds.
2. Practice ChatGPT (and image AI) deliberately or fall behind AI-fluent peers.
3. AI replaces workflows more than people—people who use AI replace those who won’t.
4. Mind/body care is part of the professional system, not a side quest.
5. Use free content and optional live masterclasses to keep learning structured.
