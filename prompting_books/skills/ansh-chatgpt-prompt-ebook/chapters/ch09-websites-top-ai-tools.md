# Chapter 9: Websites for Top AI Tools

## Core Idea
The AI product landscape moves weekly—use directories and AI news sites as a standing update habit so prompting skill stays attached to the right tools.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **AI Tool Radar Habit**: Follow a small set of directories + news outlets on a fixed cadence.
  - When to use: You feel “behind” or keep hearing tool names you don’t recognize.
  - How: Check daily-updated directories for discovery; weekly roundups for triage; news sites for capability shifts that change how you prompt.
- **Directory evaluation checklist**: When scanning a new tool listing, note features, use cases, pricing, and freshness.
  - When to use: Choosing among similar AI products.
  - How: Prefer sources that publish top features, use cases, pricing, and update frequently.
- **Time-indexed browsing**: Some directories let you navigate by month/year of discovery.
  - When to use: Researching what launched in a period or catching up after a break.
  - How: Use chronological indexes (e.g. There’s An AI For That’s past months/years lists).

## Key Concepts
- **Futurepedia (futurepedia.io)**: Large AI tools directory; daily updates; features, use cases, pricing, AI news context.
- **Eluna AI (eluna.ai)**: Weekly-updated useful/productivity-oriented tools; Instagram for daily AI news posts.
- **AI News (artificialintelligence-news.com)**: Broad AI journalism across ML, robotics, industries—“BBC of AI” metaphor in the book.
- **There’s An AI For That (theresanaiforthat.com)**: Curated tools updated daily; chronological navigation by past periods.
- **Update cadence**: Daily vs weekly sources serve different attention budgets.

## Mental Models
- Use **daily directories** when hunting a tool for a job today.
- Use **weekly digests** when you need signal without firehose noise.
- Use **AI news** when regulation, research, or industry shifts change workflows.
- Prefer **multi-source confirmation** before adopting a tool into critical work.
- Pair **tool discovery (this chapter)** with **prompt skill (Ch 8)**—tools without prompting craft underperform.

## Anti-patterns
- **Ignoring the landscape for months**: Principles stay; product fit decays.
- **Installing every new tool**: Discovery without selection criteria wastes focus.
- **News-only without hands-on trials**: Awareness ≠ capability.
- **Assuming one directory is complete**: Cross-check Futurepedia-class and There’s-An-AI-class sources.

## Worked Example
**30-minute weekly AI update ritual**:
1. **10 min — Futurepedia or There’s An AI For That**: Filter by your job (marketing, UX, writing); shortlist 2 tools.
2. **10 min — Eluna**: Skim weekly list; follow one Instagram/news item if relevant.
3. **5 min — AI News**: Read one industry piece that might change client/stakeholder expectations.
4. **5 min — Decision**: Trial one tool with a Ch 4 framed prompt; note keep/kill in Notion.

**Outcome**: Continuous awareness without full-time tool FOMO.

## Key Takeaways
1. Stay current via directories + AI news, not rumor alone.
2. Match source cadence (daily/weekly) to your attention budget.
3. Evaluate listings on features, use cases, pricing, and freshness.
4. Chronological indexes help after time away.
5. Tool awareness complements—does not replace—prompt engineering.
